**What is TENSOR?**  
TENSOR (Threat Evaluation & Neutralization through Structured Organizational Resilience) is an **open standard** for graph-based security operations. It is **not a standalone product** or software tool, but rather a framework of definitions that vendors and practitioners can integrate into their own cybersecurity platforms and processes. TENSOR provides a vendor-neutral methodology to capture and correlate investigative steps as a graph of questions and answers, enabling consistent and comprehensive incident response.

As an open standard, TENSOR is maintained by the community-led TENSOR Standards Consortium. The core framework (including schemas and the base investigative graph) is published openly for anyone to use or extend. **Security vendors** can incorporate TENSOR into their products to ensure compatibility and best-practice investigation workflows. **Security teams** can adopt TENSOR to standardize their procedures, using the common set of investigation questions and logic across tools and cases. By design, TENSOR unifies disparate tools and analysts under a shared investigation language, bridging human expertise and automation (including AI assistants) with a single, extensible model.
