## Layer example and format boundary

The vendor layer adds `Q9001` and an evidence mapping on `Q4`; the organization layer adds `Q9002` and a policy annotation on `Q4`. Each can be enabled independently. Both preserve the public question fields and original routes. Imports and exports retain distinct layer provenance. The examples are fictional and do not execute collection or policy actions.

The Atlas displays public graphs. Custom layer preview uses Graph Studio's published graph format.
